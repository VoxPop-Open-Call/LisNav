# LisNav
LisNav is an innovative app that merges **Augmented Reality (AR)** with **Artificial Intelligence (AI)** to empower and enhance the lives of the visually impaired by providing on-demand environmental audio descriptions.

  <img src="https://www.3finery.com/images/LisNav.webp" alt="LisNav Logo" />
</p>

## 🌟 Features
- 🎙️ **Audio Descriptions:** Leverages open data to provide rich audio-described content in urban environments.
- 📍 **Community-Powered:** Users can add and share points of interest within their local community.
- 🗺️ **Seamless Navigation:** Provides real-time, audio-assisted environmental information for outdoor navigation.
- 🎧 **Flexible Audio Support:** Works with both device-embedded speakers and external audio peripherals.

## 🎯 Benefits
✔️ **Navigate with Confidence** – Move freely through both familiar and unfamiliar environments.  
✔️ **Discover Points of Interest** – Access real-time audio descriptions about places around you.  
✔️ **Cutting-Edge Tech** – Experience a groundbreaking AR and AI solution built for accessibility.

## 🚀 How to Use LisNav
1. 📥 **Download & Install** – Get the [LisNav app](https://www.3finery.com/LisNav) from the official project page.
2. 🔄 **Launch the App** – Open LisNav on your device.
3. ✅ **Grant Permissions** – Allow AR and audio access for optimal performance.
4. 🌍 **Explore & Navigate** – Use the app to experience your surroundings like never before!
